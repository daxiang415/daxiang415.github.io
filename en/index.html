01 / RESEARCH

Research

Understanding energy systems. Informing their operation.

01

From prediction to decisions

Energy forecasting & control

I develop forecasting and control methods for renewable and building energy systems. My interests include data-efficient learning, model predictive control and reinforcement learning, with attention to uncertainty in operational decisions.

Time series / MPC / Reinforcement learning

02

From signals to equipment condition

HVAC fault detection & diagnosis

I use time-series data to study faults in chillers and HVAC systems. This work covers few-shot and self-supervised learning, compact models, and interpretable diagnosis when labelled fault data are limited.

Fault diagnosis / Few-shot learning / Explainable AI

03

The infrastructure behind AI

Sustainable data centres

I examine cooling and electricity use in data centres, including their energy, water and carbon implications. This research considers how climate and operating conditions inform system design and operational assessment.

Cooling / Electricity / Water & carbon
